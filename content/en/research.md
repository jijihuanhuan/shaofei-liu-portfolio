# Research

I study how redundancy in AI computation can be discovered, encoded, and converted into real system-level gains. My work spans model operators, compression, architecture simulation, and CUDA C++ integration.

## Core Directions

- AI hardware–software co-design
- Generative models and H.266/VVC video coding
- Architecture modeling and PPA evaluation
- CUDA C++ system integration and optimization

## Current Project

H.266/VVC tensor compression and compute bypass for generative models. Current work covers feature-redundancy profiling, VVenC/VVdeC data adaptation, codec-parameter search, bypass operator design, and CUDA C++ integration. Results will be published after validation.
